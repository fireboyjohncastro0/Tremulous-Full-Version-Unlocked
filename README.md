## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

Tremulous is an open-source first-person shooter (FPS) game for computers that allows players to engage in thrilling battles as either aliens or humans. Each side comes with its own unique skills and strategies, creating an engaging and competitive gameplay experience. Players must navigate through dynamic maps and use their abilities to control the operations center while attempting to outsmart their opponents. While its graphics may not be top-tier, Tremulous compensates with fast-paced action that keeps players on the edge of their seats.
